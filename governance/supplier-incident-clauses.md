# AI Supplier Incident Clauses (template)

Used in Lab 8.4 and Lab 15.2. Have your own legal adviser draft the final wording.

| Clause | What to require | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Notification window | Written notice to a named security contact within 24 to 72 hours of the supplier becoming aware of an incident involving your data or systems, including incidents caused by the supplier's own AI models or agents | A generic inbox weeks later is not notification |
| Named contacts | A named security contact on both sides, reviewed every 6 months | Messages to unmonitored inboxes are not received |
| Scope | Covers the supplier's models, agents, staff and subcontractors acting on or against your systems, not only breaches of the supplier's platform | Agentic incidents may originate inside the supplier |
| Evidence | Logs, timeline and affected data sets provided within an agreed period | You need facts to notify regulators and individuals |
| Cooperation | Support for your investigation and for any regulator or parliamentary inquiry | Your obligations do not stop at the contract boundary |
| Audit and assurance | Current SOC 2 or equivalent; for government, IRAP or equivalent evidence for the services in scope | Assurance must match the services you use |
| Exit | Data return and deletion confirmation | Incidents sometimes end relationships |
